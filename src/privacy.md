# BallerZ Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal data BallerZ collects when you play, why, who else sees it, how long we keep it and how to get it changed or deleted. BallerZ is a free online football game on Steam.

## Who we are

BallerZ is made by **nuuunu Games**, a solo developer based in Portugal: João Azevedo, who decides how your data is used (the "controller" under the GDPR).

**Contact for anything in this policy:** [nuuunugames@gmail.com](mailto:nuuunugames@gmail.com). This address is also our single point of contact under the EU Digital Services Act, for players and for authorities. You can write in English or Portuguese.

In this policy, "we" means nuuunu Games and "you" means a player of BallerZ.

## What we collect

We collect only what the game needs to run matches, clubs, leagues and the community. We never collect your real name, email address, payment details or location, and we don't use advertising or analytics trackers.

| Data | What it is | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Steam account | Your SteamID and, with Steam Family Sharing, the SteamID of the account that owns the game | Steam confirms it each time you sign in |
| Player profile | Your in-game name, country (if you pick one), ball look and shirt number, when you joined and were last online | You, in the locker room and the Community page |
| Match and career data | Results, goals, assists, saves, Man of the Match, matches played, clubs and line-ups | The game server, from official matches (friendlies, league, cup) and casual matches |
| Match recordings (demos) | A replay of official matches: everyone's positions, names, looks and quick chat calls | The game server |
| Clubs and leagues | Your club, role, applications and invites, league sign-ups and the times you mark as available | You and your club's staff |
| What you write | Room chat, club chat, shoutbox, private messages, forum posts, polls and signatures, club messages, friendly notes | You |
| What you upload | Club logos and banners | Club captains and vice-captains |
| Notifications and reading status | Which notifications and forum posts you've seen, threads you follow, players you've blocked | The game, as you use it |
| Moderation records | Reports you send or that are made about you (with the recent chat lines attached as evidence), warnings, mutes, kicks, bans and their reasons | Players, moderators and the server's automatic anti-cheat check |
| Connection data | Your IP address, used only to connect you to the server and never stored | Your connection to our server |

## Why we use it

We use your data only to run the game and keep it fair and safe. We never sell it or use it for advertising.

| Purpose | Data used | Legal basis (GDPR) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Signing you in and keeping your account | Steam account, player profile | Contract: providing the game you chose to play |
| Running matches, clubs, leagues, cups and friendlies | Profile, match data, clubs and leagues, connection data | Contract |
| Showing profiles, tables, match cards and replays to other players | Profile, match data, match recordings | Contract |
| Chat, private messages and the forum | What you write, notifications | Contract |
| Moderation: handling reports, enforcing the rules, stopping cheating, banning evasion with new accounts | Moderation records, what you write, Steam account (including the Family Sharing owner) | Legitimate interest in a safe, fair community, and our legal duties under the Digital Services Act |
| Keeping the server working and secure, fixing bugs | Connection data, server logs | Legitimate interest |
| Answering requests from authorities | Whatever the request lawfully covers | Legal obligation |

Where we rely on legitimate interest, you can object (see [Your rights](#your-rights)).

## Who else sees your data

**Other players** see what the game shows publicly: your name, ball look, country, club, career stats, match results, match recordings, forum posts, shouts and room chat. Private messages are seen only by you and the other player, and by moderators when one of you reports the conversation. Club chat is seen by your club's members.

**Service providers** that handle data for us:

- **Our hosting provider** runs the dedicated game server we rent in France, so all server-side data is stored on its machines. It only stores the data for us and may not use it for anything else.
- **Valve (Steam)** confirms who you are when you sign in. If you play with friends, Steam also shows them what you're doing (eg: "In a room") and lets them join you. Steam's own [privacy policy](https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/) covers what Valve does with your data.
- **Discord** receives a copy of each player report in a private channel only our moderators can read, so we can act on it quickly. A report includes the reported player's name and the chat lines attached as evidence. Discord is based in the USA and covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

**Moderators:** players we trust to help enforce the rules can see reports, their evidence and the moderation history of the players involved.

**Authorities:** we share data only when the law requires it, eg: a valid order from a court or the police.

We don't sell your data, share it with advertisers or use it to train AI.

## Where it's stored and how long we keep it

Your data is stored on a dedicated game server in France (European Union). Most of it is kept while your account exists; chat and recordings are cleared on their own much sooner.

| Data | How long |
| --- | --- |
| Player profile, career stats, club and league data | While your account exists. Accounts not used for 2 years are deleted automatically. |
| Match results and line-ups | As long as the game runs, so league tables and club histories stay complete. If your account is deleted, your name is removed from them. |
| Match recordings (demos) on the server | 60 days |
| Club chat | The club's latest 60 lines |
| Shoutbox | The latest 100 lines of each channel |
| Private messages | The latest 200 per conversation, and the whole conversation is deleted after 90 days without a new message |
| Forum posts, polls, signatures | Until you or a moderator delete them. A deleted post's text is erased at once. |
| Club logos and banners | Until replaced or removed, or the club is disbanded |
| Notifications | Your latest 50 |
| Reports and moderation records | Closed reports: 60 days. Warnings, mutes, bans and the moderators' action log: 2 years after they end. Active bans are kept until they end. All deleted automatically. |
| Server logs (sign-ins, matches, room chat; no IP addresses) | Up to 30 days |

When we delete data, it also leaves our backups within 30 days.

## What stays on your computer

Some things are saved only on your own PC and never sent to us:

- Your settings, key bindings and locker room choices, plus a copy of your club panel for the main menu.
- Recordings you make in casual rooms (Start Recording), goal clips you save (F8) and match demos you download, all in `Documents/BallerZ`.
- Cached club logos and banners.

You can delete these at any time; uninstalling the game removes all but the files in `Documents/BallerZ`.

A room recording can contain other players' names and their room chat. Please don't publish one in a way that harms or embarrasses them.

## Children

You must be at least **13** to play BallerZ online. If the law of your country sets a higher age for agreeing to the use of your data online (between 13 and 16 in the EU), you need a parent's or guardian's permission until you reach it.

We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you think a child under 13 is playing, email us and we'll delete the account.

To help keep younger players safe, chat and forum text is filtered for offensive words, any player can be blocked or reported, and every report is read by a person.

## Your rights

Email **[nuuunugames@gmail.com](mailto:nuuunugames@gmail.com)** from any address and tell us your in-game name; we answer within one month. To check the request comes from you, we may ask you to send a short code in the game's chat or from your Steam profile.

You can ask us to:

- **See your data:** a copy of everything we hold about you.
- **Correct it:** most things you can change yourself in the game.
- **Delete it:** press DELETE ACCOUNT on your own profile in the game, or email us. Everything is deleted at once: your account, profile, club roles, private messages, notifications and reading status. Your name becomes "Deleted player" in match results and on your forum posts, so threads stay readable (delete any post yourself first if you want it gone). Match recordings that include you are deleted within 60 days. We may keep a record of an active ban, so a ban can't be escaped by deleting the account.
- **Take it with you:** your data in a common file format (JSON).
- **Object or restrict:** stop or pause a use of your data that relies on our legitimate interest.

If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, the [CNPD](https://www.cnpd.pt), or the authority in your own country.

## Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we'll update this page and its date, and announce bigger changes in the game and on our Discord before they apply.
