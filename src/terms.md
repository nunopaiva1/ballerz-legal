You must be at least **13** to play online. If you're under the age your country sets for agreeing to the use of your data online (13 to 16 in the EU), a parent or guardian must agree to these terms for you.

Your in-game name must follow the community rules. We can reset a name that doesn't.

## Community rules

These apply everywhere in BallerZ: names, rooms, chat, private messages, the shoutbox, the forum, club pages, logos and banners.

**Not allowed:**

1. Harassment, bullying, threats, or targeting someone again and again, including after they've blocked you.
2. Hate speech or slurs about anyone's race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, sexuality or disability.
3. Sexual content, and any sexual or inappropriate contact with minors. We report this to the authorities.
4. Violent, gory or shocking content, or encouraging self-harm.
5. Sharing someone's personal information (real name, address, photos, contacts) without their permission.
6. Pretending to be another player, a moderator or nuuunu Games.
7. Spam, scams, advertising, or links to malware or phishing.
8. Cheating: modified game files, hacks, macros or bots, exploiting bugs on purpose, or anything that gives an unfair advantage. Report bugs to us instead.
9. Ruining matches on purpose: own goals, leaving or idling to sabotage your team, or fixing results in leagues and cups.
10. Getting around a ban or mute, eg: with another Steam account.
11. Uploading or posting anything you don't have the right to use (see the next section).
12. Anything illegal where you or we are.

Trash talk about the football is fine. Making it personal isn't.

You can be inspired by a style, but don't copy it.

We can remove content that breaks these terms, or that a rights holder asks us to take down.

## Reporting content

Anyone can report something that breaks these rules or the law, including people who don't play.

- **In the game:** use REPORT on a player's profile, a club page, a forum post or a room card, or type `/report NAME reason` in chat. The server attaches the recent chat lines as evidence.
- **By email:** [nuuunugames@gmail.com](mailto:nuuunugames@gmail.com). For illegal content or a copyright or trademark claim, please include where it is (player, club or post), why you think it's illegal or infringing, and your name and email, plus a statement that you believe the report is accurate. Rights holders should say which work or mark is theirs.

Every report is read by a person. We act quickly and fairly, and tell the reporter what we decided when they gave us a way to reach them. We'll never tell the reported player who reported them.

We report content suggesting a threat to someone's life or safety, or the abuse of a child, to the authorities.

## Moderation

We and our moderators can act when someone breaks these terms. We choose a step that fits what happened and how often it's happened before:

| Step | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Warning | A message in the game |
| Content removed | A post, message, club message, logo or banner is deleted, or a player or club name is reset |
| Mute | No chat, private messages, shouts or forum posts for a time |
| Kick | Removed from the server for now |
| Ban | No access to BallerZ online for a time or for good, covering the Steam account (and with Family Sharing, the account that owns the game) |

Cheating can lead straight to a ban. The game also checks for impossible kicks by itself, and its flags are reviewed by a person before anything is decided.

**We always tell you why**: what rule or law it was about, what we did and for how long, and whether it was found automatically. You'll see this in the game, or by email if you wrote to us.

**Appeals:** if you think we got it wrong, email [nuuunugames@gmail.com](mailto:nuuunugames@gmail.com) within 6 months, with your in-game name. Someone who wasn't involved in the first decision, where possible, will review it, and we'll reply with the outcome. Reporters can appeal a decision not to act the same way.

We don't read private messages unless one of the players in the conversation reports it.
