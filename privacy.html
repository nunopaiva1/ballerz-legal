BallerZ Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data BallerZ collects when you play, why, who else sees it, how long we keep it and how to get it changed or deleted. BallerZ is a free online football game on Steam.

Who we are

BallerZ is made by nuuunu Games, a solo developer based in Portugal: João Azevedo, who decides how your data is used (the "controller" under the GDPR).

Contact for anything in this policy: nuuunugames@gmail.com. This address is also our single point of contact under the EU Digital Services Act, for players and for authorities. You can write in English or Portuguese.

In this policy, "we" means nuuunu Games and "you" means a player of BallerZ.

What we collect

We collect only what the game needs to run matches, clubs, leagues and the community. We never collect your real name, email address, payment details or location, and we don't use advertising or analytics trackers.

Who else sees your data

Other players see what the game shows publicly: your name, ball look, country, club, career stats, match results, match recordings, forum posts, shouts and room chat. Private messages are seen only by you and the other player, and by moderators when one of you reports the conversation. Club chat is seen by your club's members.

Service providers that handle data for us:

  • Our hosting provider runs the dedicated game server we rent in France, so all server-side data is stored on its machines. It only stores the data for us and may not use it for anything else.
  • Valve (Steam) confirms who you are when you sign in. If you play with friends, Steam also shows them what you're doing (eg: "In a room") and lets them join you. Steam's own privacy policy covers what Valve does with your data.
  • Discord receives a copy of each player report in a private channel only our moderators can read, so we can act on it quickly. A report includes the reported player's name and the chat lines attached as evidence. Discord is based in the USA and covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Moderators: players we trust to help enforce the rules can see reports, their evidence and the moderation history of the players involved.

Authorities: we share data only when the law requires it, eg: a valid order from a court or the police.

We don't sell your data, share it with advertisers or use it to train AI.

Where it's stored and how long we keep it

Your data is stored on a dedicated game server in France (European Union). Most of it is kept while your account exists; chat and recordings are cleared on their own much sooner.

When we delete data, it also leaves our backups within 30 days.

What stays on your computer

Some things are saved only on your own PC and never sent to us:

  • Your settings, key bindings and locker room choices, plus a copy of your club panel for the main menu.
  • Recordings you make in casual rooms (Start Recording), goal clips you save (F8) and match demos you download, all in Documents/BallerZ.
  • Cached club logos and banners.

You can delete these at any time; uninstalling the game removes all but the files in Documents/BallerZ.

A room recording can contain other players' names and their room chat. Please don't publish one in a way that harms or embarrasses them.

Children

You must be at least 13 to play BallerZ online. If the law of your country sets a higher age for agreeing to the use of your data online (between 13 and 16 in the EU), you need a parent's or guardian's permission until you reach it.

We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you think a child under 13 is playing, email us and we'll delete the account.

To help keep younger players safe, chat and forum text is filtered for offensive words, any player can be blocked or reported, and every report is read by a person.

Your rights

Email nuuunugames@gmail.com from any address and tell us your in-game name; we answer within one month. To check the request comes from you, we may ask you to send a short code in the game's chat or from your Steam profile.

You can ask us to:

  • See your data: a copy of everything we hold about you.
  • Correct it: most things you can change yourself in the game.
  • Delete it: press DELETE ACCOUNT on your own profile in the game, or email us. Everything is deleted at once: your account, profile, club roles, private messages, notifications and reading status. Your name becomes "Deleted player" in match results and on your forum posts, so threads stay readable (delete any post yourself first if you want it gone). Match recordings that include you are deleted within 60 days. We may keep a record of an active ban, so a ban can't be escaped by deleting the account.
  • Take it with you: your data in a common file format (JSON).
  • Object or restrict: stop or pause a use of your data that relies on our legitimate interest.

If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, the CNPD, or the authority in your own country.

Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we'll update this page and its date, and announce bigger changes in the game and on our Discord before they apply.
